1Who we are and what this policy covers

Hanflux AI ("Hanflux", "we", "us" or "our") designs, builds and operates AI voice agents, custom AI agents, workflow automations, knowledge and retrieval systems, and Hanflux Legal, our AI offering for law firms. We are based in India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data when you:

  • visit hanflux.ai or any page that links to this policy (the "Site");
  • contact us, book a call, fill in one of our intake forms or otherwise talk to us;
  • buy services from us, or work for an organization that does;
  • sign in with Google, or connect a Google account or another third-party account, to an application or automation that we build or operate (see section 6).

In this policy, "personal data" means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual. The Site, Hanflux Legal, and the systems we build and operate are together called the "Services".

For most of the personal data described here, we decide why and how it is processed, so we are the "controller" under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). When we run systems for a client, the client usually makes those decisions and we act on its instructions. Section 5 explains how that works.

2Personal data we collect

Data you give us

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address, phone number, company and job title.
  • Booking details when you schedule a call through our Cal.com booking page: your name, email address, chosen time, time zone and any notes you add.
  • Intake and enquiry details you submit through our forms, including forms hosted on Google Forms, such as your organization, the work you want to automate and how to reach you.
  • Communications, including emails, messages, meeting notes and anything you share with us during discovery or delivery.
  • Commercial details, such as billing contacts, billing address, GST or other tax identifiers and payment history. Card and bank payments are handled by payment providers, and we do not store full card numbers.

Data collected automatically

  • Technical data. When you load the Site, our hosting provider records your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you request, the referring page and the time of each request. This is used to deliver the Site, keep it secure and fix errors.
  • The Site's navigator. Text you type into the on-site navigator is processed in your browser to take you to the right section. If we turn on AI-assisted navigation, that text is also sent to an AI model provider to interpret it, and we do not keep it. Please do not type personal or confidential information into it.

The Site does not use advertising, analytics or social media tracking cookies. Our Cookie Policy has the details.

Data from other sources

  • Connected accounts. When you authorize access to a Google account or another service, we receive the data covered by the permissions you grant. Section 6 covers Google user data.
  • Clients. Clients give us information about their staff, customers and contacts when they need it for an engagement.
  • Public business information. When we research organizations we might work with, we use business contact details published on company websites and professional profiles.

Sensitive data

We do not ask for sensitive personal data such as health information, financial account numbers, biometric data, government identification numbers or passwords. Please do not send it to us unless we have agreed in writing how it will be handled.

4AI models and your data

The systems we build use AI models, such as Anthropic's Claude models, through business API services. When personal data is sent to a model provider:

  • we use services whose commercial terms do not allow the provider to use our inputs or outputs to train its models;
  • we send only the data the task needs, and we redact or leave out identifiers where the workflow allows it;
  • for Hanflux Legal deployments that run on a client's own servers, the model runs inside the client's infrastructure and data is not sent to an outside model provider.

We do not use client data, the contents of your communications with us, or Google user data to train or fine-tune AI models. The one exception is a model a client asks us, in writing, to tune on that client's own data for that client's sole use. Google user data is never used for this, under any circumstances.

5Data we process on behalf of clients

When we build or run a system for a client, such as a voice agent that answers the client's phone line or an automation that processes the client's email, the client decides what personal data the system handles and why. In that role we act as the client's processor. We:

  • process that data only on the client's documented instructions and for no purpose of our own;
  • bind everyone who works on it to confidentiality;
  • apply the security measures in section 10;
  • use sub-processors only under written terms that protect the data at least as well as this policy;
  • help the client respond to requests from individuals and to security incidents;
  • delete or return the data when the engagement ends, as section 9 describes.

Voice agents. Where a client turns it on, calls handled by a voice agent are recorded and transcribed so the agent can work and the client can review calls. The client is responsible for telling callers about recording and AI use and for getting any consent the law requires.

On-premise deployments. When Hanflux Legal or another system runs on a client's own servers, the data stays in the client's environment. We access it only as the client permits, for setup, support and maintenance.

If you are a customer or employee of one of our clients, the client's own privacy notice explains how your data is used, and you should send requests to the client first. If you contact us, we will pass your request to the client and help it respond. Clients can ask us for a data processing agreement at hanfluxai@gmail.com.

6Google user data

This section applies when you sign in with Google, or connect a Google account to an application or automation that Hanflux builds or operates, through Google's OAuth consent screen. An example is an automation that reads new emails in Gmail and files their attachments into the right Google Drive folder.

Hanflux AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we access

We request only the permissions (scopes) shown on Google's consent screen, and only the ones the workflow you are approving needs. Depending on the workflow, this can include:

  • your basic profile information: name, email address and profile picture;
  • Gmail: reading message metadata, message content and attachments, and applying labels, for the messages the workflow handles;
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: creating, reading, moving, renaming and updating the files and folders the workflow works with;
  • Google Calendar: reading availability and creating or updating events;
  • Google Forms: reading responses to forms that you own and connect.

Where Google offers a narrower scope that still lets the workflow run, we use it.

How we use it

We use Google user data only to provide the features you authorized, inside the application or workflow you approved. For example, we read an incoming email to decide which Drive folder its attachment belongs in, or read your calendar to book a meeting in a free slot.

What we never do with it

  • sell it, or transfer it to data brokers or information resellers;
  • use it for advertising, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based ads;
  • use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models;
  • transfer it to anyone else, except as needed to provide the features you authorized (for example, to the automation platform that runs your workflow), to comply with applicable law, for security purposes, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets with notice to you.

Human access

Our team does not read your Google user data unless you give us explicit permission to look at specific messages or files (for example, to troubleshoot a workflow), we need to do so for security purposes such as investigating abuse, we must do so to comply with the law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

Storage and protection

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in the credential store of the platform that runs the workflow, and Google user data is protected with encryption in transit (TLS). We keep only the Google user data the workflow needs, for as long as it needs it.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke our access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or by emailing hanfluxai@gmail.com. When access is revoked or the engagement ends, we delete the stored tokens and the Google user data we hold within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8International transfers

We are based in India, and some of our service providers process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Transfers out of India follow the DPDP Act, which allows them except to countries the Government of India restricts by notification.

When the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we transfer personal data out of the EEA or UK only on the basis of an adequacy decision, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum where relevant), or another mechanism the law recognizes. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply.

10How we protect personal data

We use technical and organizational measures suited to the risk, including:

  • encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest on the platforms we use;
  • multi-factor authentication and least-privilege access to systems that hold personal data;
  • encrypted storage for API keys, OAuth tokens and other credentials;
  • separate environments for each client where the project allows it;
  • logging and monitoring of the systems we operate;
  • reviewing the security and privacy terms of providers before we use them;
  • confidentiality obligations for everyone who works with us.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires. That includes the Data Protection Board of India, and the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where the GDPR applies.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights.

Everyone

You can ask to access, correct, update or delete your personal data, withdraw consent you have given, and stop receiving marketing from us.

India

Under the DPDP Act you can ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities involved, and the identities of others we have shared it with; ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it; use our grievance process in section 16; and nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated. If you are not satisfied with how we handle a grievance, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection (including to processing based on legitimate interests and to direct marketing at any time). You can also withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

United States

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws can request to know, access, correct and delete their personal information, and cannot be discriminated against for doing so. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define the terms, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a valid opt-out request. You can use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

How to make a request

Email hanfluxai@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy request". We may need to verify your identity before we act. We respond within 30 days, and we will tell you if we need longer where the law allows it. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive. If we hold your data on behalf of a client, we will forward your request to the client.

12Marketing communications
