1Who this policy applies to

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to anyone who uses hanflux.ai, and to our clients and their users of the systems Hanflux AI builds, hosts or operates, including voice agents, AI agents, chat interfaces, workflow automations, knowledge systems and Hanflux Legal (the "Systems").

The AUP forms part of our Terms of Service. Clients are responsible for making sure their staff, contractors and end users follow it. Words with capital letters have the meanings given in the Terms.

2Follow the law

Do not use the Systems for any illegal purpose, to help anyone else act illegally, or to infringe the rights of others. You must follow every law that applies to how you use the Systems, including laws on privacy and data protection, consumer protection, marketing, telecommunications, call recording, accessibility and artificial intelligence.

3Calls, messages and outreach

Voice agents and automated outreach are regulated in most countries. When a System calls, messages or emails people for you, you must:

  • get, and keep a record of, every consent the law requires before contact is made. This includes prior express written consent where United States rules on calls using artificial or AI-generated voices apply;
  • follow India's Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018, including the National Customer Preference Register (DND) and registered headers and templates for commercial messages, and the equivalent rules elsewhere, such as the US National Do Not Call Registry, the UK's Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, CAN-SPAM and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation;
  • act on opt-out requests promptly and keep suppression lists up to date;
  • contact people only at hours the law permits, and show accurate caller identification. Caller ID spoofing is prohibited;
  • never use the Systems for robocall campaigns, bulk unsolicited messages or artificially generated call traffic.

4Transparency, voices and impersonation

  • Tell people they are dealing with an AI system wherever the law requires it, for example under the transparency rules of the EU AI Act. A voice or chat agent must answer truthfully whenever someone sincerely asks whether they are talking to a human.
  • Do not use a System to impersonate a real person, company or government body, or to mislead anyone about who a communication comes from.
  • Do not clone or imitate a real person's voice or likeness without that person's documented, informed consent.
  • Announce call recording, and get consent where the law requires every party to a call to agree.

5Prohibited content and activities

Do not use the Systems to create, store or send, or to take part in:

  • child sexual abuse material or any sexualization of minors. We report this to the authorities;
  • harassment, threats, stalking, hate speech, or promotion of violence or violent extremism;
  • fraud, scams, phishing, pretexting or other social engineering, fake reviews or deceptive debt collection;
  • malware, credential theft, or attacks on any system or network;
  • content designed to mislead voters, or political campaign calls or messages made without the authorizations the law requires;
  • surveillance or tracking of individuals without a lawful basis, and any biometric categorization or emotion recognition the law prohibits;
  • discrimination that is unlawful under applicable law;
  • sexual content involving anyone who has not consented to it.

6High-impact decisions and safety

Do not use a System to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on people, such as decisions about credit, employment, housing, insurance, education, healthcare, legal matters, immigration or access to essential services, unless there is meaningful human review, a lawful basis, and any assessment or notice the law requires.

The Systems are not designed for emergencies or life-safety uses. A voice agent is not a substitute for emergency services, and callers who need urgent help must be directed to them. Any such use needs safeguards we have agreed in writing.

7Sensitive data

Unless we have agreed in writing on the safeguards and the lawful basis, do not put the following into a System:

  • payment card data, unless the workflow uses a component that complies with PCI DSS;
  • government identification numbers, such as Aadhaar, PAN or passport numbers;
  • health, genetic or biometric data;
  • passwords or other authentication secrets belonging to someone else;
  • personal data about children.

8Security and integrity

  • Do not probe, scan or test the vulnerability of any System without our written permission. If you find a vulnerability, report it to hanfluxai@gmail.com.
  • Do not bypass authentication, rate limits, usage caps or safety controls, and do not use prompt injection or similar techniques against Systems or data you are not authorized to control.
  • Do not share credentials or API keys with anyone who is not authorized, or place an unreasonable load on any System.
  • Do not reverse engineer or extract Hanflux Materials, except where the law allows it.

9Third-party rules

You must also follow the usage policies of the Third-Party Services your Systems use. These include the usage policies of the AI model provider, such as Anthropic's Usage Policy, the Google API Services User Data Policy, and the rules of the automation platforms, telecom carriers and messaging platforms involved, such as WhatsApp Business.

10Enforcement and reporting

We may investigate suspected breaches of this AUP. If we find one, we may remove content, refuse instructions, suspend or disable a System or feature, end the relevant Services under our Terms, and cooperate with the authorities. Where we reasonably can, we will tell you first and give you a chance to fix the problem. We may act without notice when a breach is serious or urgent.

To report misuse of a System we built or operate, email hanfluxai@gmail.com with the subject line "Abuse report".

11Changes to this policy

We may update this AUP as laws, technology and risks change. We will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and tell clients about material changes before they take effect.
