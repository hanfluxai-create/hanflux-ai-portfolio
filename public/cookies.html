1What this policy covers

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and scripts that read or store information on your device. This Cookie Policy explains how they are used on hanflux.ai (the "Site"). It forms part of our Privacy Policy.

2Cookies we set

The Site does not set any cookies of its own. It does not use advertising, analytics, session replay or social media tracking technologies, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

3Third parties involved when you visit

A few third-party services are involved when you load or use the Site.

ProviderWhenWhat it does
VercelEvery visitHosts and delivers the Site. It processes your IP address and request details in server logs for security and performance, and does not set tracking cookies on the Site. Vercel privacy policy
Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry)Every visitDelivers the Clash Display and Satoshi typefaces. Your browser requests the font files directly from Fontshare, which shares your IP address and browser details with it. fontshare.com
Cal.comOnly when you open the booking calendarShows our availability and takes your booking. Cal.com may set cookies or use local storage on its own domain to make booking work. Cal.com privacy policy

4Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking Cal.com's cookies may stop the booking calendar from working, in which case you can email hanfluxai@gmail.com to arrange a call instead.

We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. The Site does neither, so no further action is needed.

5Changes to this policy

If we add analytics or any other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before setting them. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
